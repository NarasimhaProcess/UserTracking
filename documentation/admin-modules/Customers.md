# Customer Management

This document describes the customer management section within the Admin screen.

## Overview
This section allows administrators to view and manage customer data, including adding transactions and viewing customer locations on a map.

## Functionality
*   **View Customers:** Displays a list of all customers with details like name, mobile, email, card number, type, and status.
*   **Customer Actions:** Provides options to call, SMS, or WhatsApp a customer.
*   **Transaction Management:** View and expand customer transactions, including total amounts.
*   **Share Details:** Share customer details or transaction lists as CSV files.
*   **Activate/Close Account:** Manage customer account status.
*   **Location Integration:** View customer locations on a map and get directions.
